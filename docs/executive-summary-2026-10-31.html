2. Why GEMSDOE32 (0.2778) scored highest

COMPUTED The file is the end of a subset chain. Each step removes dots near the labelled catalogue:

  • D2.8 (GEMSDOE30 / GEMSDOE25, 44,090 dots): 14.6% of dots within 200 m of the catalogue, owner-reported 0.2600.
  • h27-4 (GEMSDOE31, 40,199 dots): 6.33%, owner-reported 0.2708.
  • B2 (GEMSDOE32, 37,654 dots): 0.0% within 200 m, owner-reported 0.2778. It is also zeros outside the footprint, the form the portal accepts.

INFERENCE DTI (src/gems25/metric.py) weights false positives at alpha = 0.2 and false negatives at beta = 0.8, so a wrong dot costs less than a missed fault. Removing dots that sit near the catalogue can therefore raise DTI when they are false positives. Not proven: the removal could be a density effect (fewer dots), and the 0.0070 gain over the parent is within what one leaderboard round can move. Our holdout (E4) cannot separate the two: repulsion minus density-matched thinning = -0.00009 (dev) and -0.00010 (confirm), both CIs spanning zero.
