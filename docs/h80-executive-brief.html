2 · Why GEMSDOE32 B2 scored 0.2778 — and what it would take to beat 0.4413

  1. The metric pays for coverage, not for pixels. DTI = TPw / (0.2(TPw+FPw) + 0.8|G|), where a truth pixel is credited by the maximum 300-m triangular kernel over nearby predictions, while false positives are charged linearly per emitting pixel (metric). Thick ribbons therefore waste mass; isolated dots with ~2.8 px spacing keep the kernel maximum near 1.0 and cut FP mass by ~64%.
  2. B2 is the third pruning step of one single detector. H19-5 (0.1922) → D1.5 (0.2477) → D2.8 (0.2600) → R1 (0.2708) → B2 (0.2778): each step only deletes pixels, and each deletion raises the score. B2 = D2.8 minus the 6,436 dots within 200 m of the mapped catalogue, i.e. it removes dots that can never be credited because mapped faults are masked (staff clarification).
  3. The ceiling of that family is ~0.28. Four independent, already-scored rasters with the same geometry sit at 0.2708–0.2778 (gemsdoe27-h31-1). Emission tuning is exhausted; the residual gap to 0.3195/0.4413 can only come from a better detector of blind basin faults, which is what H80 adds.
  4. What H80 changes. A single planar-edge detector (the whole H19-5/H33 family) is blind by construction to fault intersections: the structure-tensor coherence it maximises collapses exactly where two strikes meet. H80 scores the complementary quantity — the Förstner bimodal junction invariant — plus multi-geothermometer boiling disequilibrium, a moisture-invariant Aitchison compositional jump, and an N≈0 structural-index filter. All four are Quaternary-catalogue-free, so they are judged on blind basins rather than on the apron of mapped traces.

8 · Limitations and flagged irregularities

  • Literal lane rule. No dense file can clear the 70%-within-3-px rule against r13-lattice-s5; the group's scored best cannot either. Reported, not waived.
  • No organizer receipt. 0.2778 / 0.3195 / 0.4413 remain owner-reported; this brief does not upgrade them.
  • Proxy validity. The holdout withholds catalogue faults, not the hidden test faults. Proxy–live rank correlation is weak (+0.33, n.s., 24 artefacts).
  • Own-lineage risk. H80 shares static physics columns with earlier H74/H75/H79 files; the final-dot audit against those is reported explicitly and is the binding constraint that shaped this emission.
  • Not delivered here. The two externally-dependent hypotheses (drainage/knickpoint routing needing the full 1-m 3DEP DEM mosaic, and InSAR × 3-D MT needing ASF/IRIS data) are documented in the pre-registration and blocked by network allowlists, not by method.
