Local audit of the reported 0.2477 raster (score unverified)

  1. The mask is exactly dot_thin(H19-5, 1.5) — pixel-for-pixel a deterministic subset of the H19-5-labelled raster: 60,069 of 121,131 pixels kept (49.6 %), none on a catalogue pixel, zero NaN inside the footprint. This verifies the raster transformation, not the reported DTI.
  2. Metric arithmetic: DTI = TPw / (0.2·(TPw+FPw) + 0.8·|G|). Thinning keeps ≈85 % of the geometric credit estimate while removing ≈50 % of the emitted pixels.
  3. Unverified score inputs: the 0.0904 lattice and 0.1922→0.2477 values are supplied/owner-reported claims. Under those assumptions, estimated |G| is ≈12,503 px and each emitted pixel needs ≥0.052 credit per unit of false-positive mass. No organizer page or receipt was accessed.

Details, tables and caveats on the Research page. Evidence: evidence/why_0_2477.json; score claims: registry/live_scores.json.

The live metric, calibrated — and the ceiling of the current file

  1. The hidden truth cannot be uniform. For four of the group's own surfaces the published DTI has no solution in the truth count under a uniform truth. Under pi ∝ exp(-d(H19-5)/1.85 px) all five anchors imply one count — lattice-s5 12,498, h19-5 12,472, h19-4 12,893, h16-1 13,358, d1-5 12,691 — so N = 12,691 hidden truth pixels (spread 5.3 %), agreeing with the blind-lattice-only estimate 12,348 and the earlier independent 12,503.
  2. It reproduces live scores. predicted vs reported: lattice-s5 0.0914 / 0.0904 · h19-5 0.1903 / 0.1922 · d1-5 0.2437 / 0.2477. Monte-Carlo of the published metric agrees with the analytic expectation to ≤0.0020.
  3. The downloadable file is the ceiling of its own family. Sweeping dot_thin(H19-5, d) over d ∈ [1.8, 4.0] peaks at d = 2.4 px, 44,090 px → 0.25104, which is exactly this D2.8 file. A kernel-disjoint 6 px design (zero redundancy) reaches only 0.19332 and beats d1-5 in 0 of 9 sensitivity cells.
  4. The remaining gap is detection, not geometry. Reaching the reported #1 (0.3195) at this budget needs 1.29× the credit density (0.1400 vs 0.1084 per emitted pixel). The 30-arm holdout factorial agrees: spacing 2.4–3.0 px is optimal, 6 px costs 0.045 DTI, habitat ranking adds +0.0003 (p = 0.25) → gate FAIL, no slot, nothing new packaged.

Which feature families matter? (designed experiment, not hunches)

A 25−1 Resolution V fractional factorial (16 runs × 8 spatially blocked hide-and-recover cells) estimates every family's main effect and all ten pairwise interactions. Main effect = change in mean sparse DTI when a family is included. Lenth ME at α = .05: 0.0139.
