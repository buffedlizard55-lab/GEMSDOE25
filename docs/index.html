Methodology — Factorial Experiment vs. OFAT

Why This Approach

The repo's hypothesis history — "conj_alteration_mag," "dem10-scarp," "topo-geophys-x-complexity-prior" — reads as classical one-factor-at-a-time (OFAT) testing. Box, Hunter, and Hunter's Statistics for Experimenters shows why OFAT systematically misses interaction effects: cases where two features are each weak alone but jointly diagnostic because the real signature is their conjunction.

This submission replaces OFAT with a 2(5-1) fractional factorial design across 5 feature families:

FactorFamilyLayersDescription
APotential-field gradientsgravity slope, TMI slope, vertical/horizontal gradientsBasement structure detection via geopotential anomalies
BDEM curvature/scarpcurvature, openness, local relief, scarp detectionSurface expression of Quaternary faults
CStrain/seismicitydilatation, shear strain, earthquake densityActive tectonic deformation markers
DThermal/geochemicalsprings, wells, geothermometers, conduit inversionGeothermal system surface expressions
ECatalogue-geometrydistance to faults, map-scale corridorsPositional uncertainty and completeness modeling

Design Resolution: V (25-1, 16 runs)

Generator: E = ABCD · Defining relation: I = ABCDE

• All main effects clear of two-factor interactions

• Two-factor interactions aliased only with three-factor interactions (negligible under sparsity-of-effects)

• Estimates all 5 main effects and 10 pairwise interactions in just 16 runs
