Why B2 can work — conditional explanation, not a verified score cause

Pixel forensics show B2 is exactly the D2.8 mask after 6,436 predictions within 200 m of the supplied catalogue are removed. The owner-reported claim increases 0.2600 → 0.2708 → 0.2778 through catalogue collars of 100 m then 200 m; removing 1,200 extra dots to make GEMSDOE36 accompanies a lower owner-reported 0.2750.

Under the repo's DTI form, a prediction receives credit within a 300 m triangular kernel while false-positive mass is charged. Pruning can improve the ratio if the removed collar dots carry less marginal credit than their false-positive cost; the hidden labels are unavailable, so the mechanism is an inference conditional on reported scores—not a proof of the true holdout mechanism.

Under the local inverse calculation's assumed hidden-positive count (12,691 pixels) and conditional score claims, D2.8→R1 deletes 3,891 dots with an implied mean credit loss of 0.000961 per deleted pixel, and R1→B2 deletes another 2,545 dots at 0.005029 per deleted pixel. Both are below the algebraic break-even thresholds in that assumed model. These are conditional model quantities, not HOLDOUT-DTI or organizer scores.

How a submission becomes eligible

  1. Research gate. Beat the same-run best comparable buffered holdout; report pooled HOLDOUT-DTI, evaluator version, withheld-positive count and 95% CI. Confirm on fresh draws.
  2. Novelty gate. Check the surface before placement and exact final dots against the registry. Stop for rank correlation above 0.90 or more than 70% within 3 px. Renaming, changing compression or filling outside NaNs is not a new prediction.
  3. File gate. One float32 GeoTIFF band, finite predictions in [0,1] throughout the template footprint, identical CRS, shape and geotransform. Use the shared writer; reject invalid data instead of silently clipping it.
  4. Selector gate. Separate authorization and a verified remaining weekly slot are required. The software must not infer either from a format-pass receipt.
  5. Only after all gates pass: download the exact approved hash-linked TIF, open the competition submission form, select that TIF (not this report or JSON), paste its ≤140-character note and save the organizer receipt. This project does not automate upload.
