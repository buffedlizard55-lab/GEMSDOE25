### Why the reported B2 score might be higher (conditional explanation, not causal proof)

The local DTI formulation credits a truth location by its best nearby prediction while accumulating false-positive prediction mass. If pruning catalogue-nearby dots removes substantial redundant false-positive mass and little hidden-fault credit, the metric can increase. That is a plausible mechanism for B2 relative to its parent-like D2.8 mask; it does not demonstrate that those pruned locations contain no hidden faults, and it does not establish that a new method can beat 0.2778. The score is not a causal validation of any geological mechanism.
