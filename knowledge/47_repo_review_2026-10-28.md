## What the 0.2778 result does and does not show

The competition target is fault detection, not direct geothermal-vent identification (the pinned official reference-solution notebook describes the task as fault detection). Hydrothermal/vent evidence can at most inform a structural prior; predicted fault pixels must not be labelled as vent probabilities without a separate target and validation dataset.

The pinned mirror audit identifies GEMSDOE32 B2 as 37,654 positive pixels and shows it equals the GEMSDOE25/GEMSDOE30 D2.8 mask after removing 6,436 pixels within two pixels (200 m on the 100 m grid) of the mirrored catalogue. The local DTI objective credits truth by a maximum distance-weighted contribution while false-positive mass accumulates over emitted pixels. Therefore, sparsification and selective pruning can plausibly improve the local coverage/false-positive tradeoff. That is a mechanism-based explanation, **not evidence that the removed dots are false positives on hidden truth**, not a causal result, and not a transferable score forecast. The GEMSDOE36 file adds 1,200 B2 pixels and its supplied score is 0.2750; several other historical masks are exact duplicates or related prunings. See the per-site table for all nine.

The user-supplied overall leaderboard values 0.3195 and 0.4413 conflict. The current feed therefore cannot be autonomously refreshed from DrivenData under the standing policy; a permitted owner-provided page receipt/export is the remaining way to verify live leaderboard status. ### Gate order before any experiment or submission

1. Obtain an explicit, single method-lane assignment and exact holdout plan; audit related methods in the named sibling repositories first.
2. Restore and hash-verify the shared template cache. This checkout has only `data/.gitkeep`; the experiment cannot be reproduced from current local inputs.
3. Use shared `evaluate_holdout.py`/`evaluate_holdout` and `submission_writer.py`/`submission.py` code paths (not a private implementation). Withhold entire fault components with a buffer; derive catalogue features from visible faults only; mask visible catalogue cells exactly; run per-feature AUC canaries (any AUC >0.90 is leakage until resolved); compare against same-run controls using pooled DTI and clustered 95% CIs.
4. Before placement, check the continuous surface against every aligned registry raster. After placement, check final dots against every registry raster. `|Spearman| > 0.90` or >70% of candidate dots within 3 px is a stop, not a reason to rename/repackage.
5. Validate exact file bytes: one-band float32, no NaN/Inf anywhere, values in [0,1], catalogue mask exact, and CRS/shape/transform identical to the template. A format pass does not imply score or slot readiness.
6. Only after the pre-registered candidate beats the same-run current holdout best and passes the full lane/readiness audit may a separate selector consider a real weekly slot. No automatic upload and no automated DrivenData access.
