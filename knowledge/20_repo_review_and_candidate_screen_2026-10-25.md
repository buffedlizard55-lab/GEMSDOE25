## What the highest reported GEMSDOE32 artifact did

`h33-h33-2-b2-20261004T220000Z-e5eb6e7e-zeros` has an **OWNER-REPORTED score of 0.2778**; no organizer receipt was accessed in this review. Repository forensics recorded in `evidence/top_scorer_forensics.json` reconstructs its pixels exactly as the GEMSDOE28/31 `h27-4` mask with every prediction within 2 pixels (200 m at the recorded 100 m cell size) of the fault catalogue removed. The prior chain is: score-blind dotting of the H19-5 surface, then catalogue-distance pruning. This is a computed raster relationship, not proof of the hidden-label mechanism or of score causality.

The best-supported interpretation is an *emission-efficiency* effect: under the repository's triangular 300 m DTI kernel, redundant or low-marginal-credit dots can add false-positive mass while earning little new true-positive credit. A distance-pruning step can therefore improve DTI if the removed points' marginal credit is below the DTI break-even. That interpretation is not validated against hidden labels: the hide-and-recover target consists of withheld catalogue components, and cannot establish whether the real test labels are depleted near mapped traces. The submission's owner-reported score, the exact pixel transformation, and a causal explanation are three different evidence claims.

The project has **not demonstrated a score above 0.2778**. The KDC attempt failed its preregistered same-run holdout gate and the literal lane threshold; the strict lane rule is absolute. Its tracked unique file remains useful for offline testing only. Do not modify or reinterpret the lane threshold to make it pass.
