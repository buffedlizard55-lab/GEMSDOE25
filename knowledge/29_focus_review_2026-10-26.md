## Why the reported GEMSDOE32 B2 value is 0.2778

**Classification first:** 0.2778 is an **OWNER-REPORTED DTI claim**, not an organizer-confirmed score in this repository. The exact file bytes and geometry can be verified locally; the claimed score cannot be authenticated without a submission-page receipt.

The local pixel lineage is exact: the GEMSDOE25/GEMSDOE30 D2.8 mask (44,090 dots; claimed 0.2600) is thinned by removing pixels within 100 m of the supplied visible catalogue to form R1 (40,199 dots; claimed 0.2708), then within 200 m to form B2 (37,654 dots; claimed 0.2778). This is a mask identity, not an independent score measurement. The G36 mask is B2 plus 1,200 pixels and its supplied claim is lower at 0.2750.

A conditional inversion of the repo's metric using a model-implied hidden-positive count of 12,691 and the reported score claims estimates mean credit lost per deleted pixel as 0.000961 for the first 3,891-dot collar and 0.005029 for the next 2,545 dots. Those are far below the corresponding algebraic break-even ratios (0.054852 and 0.057261) under that model. The same calculation estimates the 1,200 extra G36 pixels at 0.013422 credit per pixel, below its conditional break-even ratio of 0.058829. This is consistent with the reported pruning/extension pattern: deleting low-marginal-credit dots can improve a Tversky-style metric because it reduces false-positive mass faster than it reduces useful kernel coverage. Every value in this paragraph is conditional on the assumed hidden truth and owner-reported score anchors; none is HOLDOUT-DTI or organizer evidence.

The inference does **not** prove that a blind fault is physically absent within 200 m of known faults or that the competition's hidden-label generation uses this mechanism. The local hide-and-recover proxy hides catalogue components and cannot evaluate the B2 catalogue-distance pruning premise itself (`IR-25-HOLDOUT-DOES-NOT-TEST-PRUNING`). A genuinely better file would need new high-credit fault pixels not already covered by B2 while avoiding high false-positive mass. The latest H71 result does not provide that evidence: its HOLDOUT-DTI is 0.050577, versus same-run H35 mean 0.151033 (paired mean gain −0.100457; 0/4 folds positive), and it triggers the literal r13 lattice lane stop. No defensible live-score or >0.2778 projection is available.
