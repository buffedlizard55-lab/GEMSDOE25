## 1. Why `h33-h33-2-b2-…-e5eb6e7e-zeros` (0.2778, OWNER-reported) is the group best

Decomposition already established in knowledge/17 from this repo's own pixel
forensics and the owner-reported score chain:

| Step | Change | OWNER DTI | Δ |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| solid h19-5 multiline surface (~121k px) | detector | 0.1922 | — |
| score-blind geodesic dotting d1.5 → d2.8 budget (44,090 px) | emission | 0.2477 → 0.2600 | +0.068 |
| h27-4 base surface at the same budget | detector | 0.2708 | +0.011 |
| delete every dot within 2 px (200 m) of the catalogue (37,654 dots) | emission/deletion | 0.2778 | +0.007 |

PhD-level reading: under DTI = TPw/(TPw + 0.2·FPw + 0.8·FNw) with max-kernel
credit, the second dot within 300 m of a truth pixel adds ≈0 credit but a full
FP unit. Dotting is therefore pure emission economics and bought the largest
gain in the group's history. A dot survives at D=0.2778 only if its marginal
credit/FP ratio exceeds 0.0588 (`gems25.metric.marginal_inclusion_ratio`) — the
B2 catalogue-flank deletion paid *iff* flank dots earn below that bar, which is
plausible if hidden truth near mapped traces is mostly already covered, but is
**not proven**: it is one artefact, its own site projected 0.2747, and it sits
in tension with the staff clarification that test truth may include corrections
within 300 m of known traces (registry/sources.json `dd-forum-11516`).
Leaderboard claims in the brief conflict (0.4413 vs 0.3195 vs GEMSDOE32's
0.3262 read) — IR-25-LEADERBOARD-CONFLICT remains open; all are OWNER claims here.

**Can anything in this repo beat 0.2778 today? Not demonstrated.** At constant
mass, +0.01 needs ≈+4% mean credit per emitted pixel and 0.3195 needs ≈+25%.
Emission-only tricks are exhausted in this repo's evidence; the only lever with
headroom is a better detector. That is what this session tested — twice — and
both tests were negative (below). A projection is never written as a score.

## 4. Experiment 2 — H37-SEISALIGN: NEGATIVE

Pre-registered knowledge/22. Screen, draws 40–41, 11,036 withheld positives:
BEST **0.1609** (0.1413–0.1805); SEIS **0.1588** (0.1413–0.1762); paired gain
**−0.0021** (CI −0.0074…+0.0031), 1/4 folds. Canary worst AUC 0.536 — the
alignment columns are individually near-random on this proxy (0.46–0.52), so
the negative is a signal absence, not leakage. Gate **FAILED** → no confirmation.
Evidence: `evidence/h37_screen/`.

Interpretation: adding a weak, diffuse physics block on top of a strong base
detector mostly re-ranks dots the base already placed well; the paired CIs
include small positive values, so these are "no demonstrated gain" results,
not proofs of zero effect. The mimic risk named in advance (lithologic
contacts for TZC; kernel azimuth/seismicity artefacts for SEIS) remains a
further reason not to spend a slot on either.
