## 3. Frozen Mathematical Specification of `H38-1` (`OSPC × SUBMOD` $2^2$ Factorial)

### 3.1 Factor `G` (`H38-OSPC` Static Sidecar: `src/gems25/h38.py`, 4 `float32` columns)

All four columns are computed **strictly from static, catalogue-free layers** (`work/bands/{02_rtp,13_iso_grav_anom,17_cond_surf}.npy` and `external/lidar_scarp_features_u8.tif`) and stored as `work/h38_ospc_columns.npy` of shape `(4, 5_167_373)`. No label, `existing_faults.tif`, or `dist_known_fault_px` column is ever read.

1. **Dequantization of `lidar_scarp_features_u8.tif`** (identical to `src/gems25/features.py:35–42`):
   - `valid` $V_L = \mathbb{I}(q_{11} > 0)$ (`float32` in `{0.0, 1.0}`); all LiDAR channels zeroed where $V_L = 0$.
   - $D = (q_5 / 255)^2 \cdot 1.0 \cdot V_L$ (`downface_max`), $U = (q_6 / 255)^2 \cdot 1.0 \cdot V_L$ (`upface_max`), $E_{\text{mean}} = (q_1 / 255)^2 \cdot 0.3 \cdot V_L$ (`ex_mean`), $S_{\text{step}} = (q_2 / 255)^2 \cdot 1.0 \cdot V_L$ (`step_max`), $C_{100} = (q_9 / 255) \cdot V_L$ (`coh100`).
   - Strike angle $\theta_L = \text{deg2rad}((q_{10} / 255) \cdot 180.0)$; spin-2 components $c_{2,L} = \cos(2\theta_L) V_L$, $s_{2,L} = \sin(2\theta_L) V_L$ (matching `features.py:153–155`, where $x = \text{col}$, $y = \text{row}$).
2. **`H38_monoclinal_polarity`:**
   $$P_{\text{mono}} = \frac{|D - U|}{D + U + 10^{-4}} \cdot E_{\text{mean}} \cdot C_{100} \cdot V_L$$
3. **`H38_meso_scarp_coh` ($\sigma_{\text{meso}} = 6.0\text{ px} = 600\text{ m}$):**
   Let $w_L = S_{\text{step}} \cdot C_{100} \cdot V_L$, $M_c = G_{6.0}(w_L c_{2,L})$, $M_s = G_{6.0}(w_L s_{2,L})$, $M_0 = G_{6.0}(w_L)$:
   $$C_{\text{meso}} = \text{clip}\!\left(\frac{\sqrt{M_c^2 + M_s^2}}{M_0 + 10^{-6}},\, 0,\, 1\right), \qquad \text{H38\_meso\_scarp\_coh} = G_{6.0}(P_{\text{mono}}) \cdot C_{\text{meso}}$$
4. **`H38_subsurf_strike_align`:**
   Let $\bar{c}_{2,\text{surf}} = M_c / (\sqrt{M_c^2 + M_s^2} + 10^{-12})$, $\bar{s}_{2,\text{surf}} = M_s / (\sqrt{M_c^2 + M_s^2} + 10^{-12})$.
   For $f_g = \text{nearest\_fill}(\text{iso\_grav\_anom})$ and $f_m = \text{RTP}_f - \text{RTP}_{\uparrow 400\text{m}}$ (`gems25.h34.upward_continue_multi`, $dz = 400\text{ m}$), compute smoothed gradients $(g_y, g_x) = \nabla G_{2.0}(f)$, normalize magnitude $\tilde{m} = \text{clip}(\sqrt{g_x^2 + g_y^2} / P_{90,\text{foot}},\, 0,\, 3)$, and form each field's contour-tangent spin-2 vector $\tilde{m} \cdot \left(\frac{g_y^2 - g_x^2}{g_x^2 + g_y^2 + 10^{-12}},\, \frac{-2 g_x g_y}{g_x^2 + g_y^2 + 10^{-12}}\right)$ (matching `features.py:249–250`). Sum the two normalized subsurface spin-2 vectors to $(U_c, U_s)$, unit-normalize to $(c_{2,\text{sub}}, s_{2,\text{sub}}) = (U_c, U_s) / (\sqrt{U_c^2 + U_s^2} + 10^{-12})$, and define:
   $$\text{H38\_subsurf\_strike\_align} = \big(\bar{c}_{2,\text{surf}} c_{2,\text{sub}} + \bar{s}_{2,\text{surf}} s_{2,\text{sub}}\big) \cdot C_{\text{meso}} \in [-1, 1]$$
5. **`H38_mag_destruction_trough`:**
   $$\text{H38\_mag\_destruction\_trough} = \text{ridge\_strength}(-\text{RTP}_{\text{shallow}},\, \sigma=2.0) \cdot \text{pct\_norm}_{\text{foot}, 1\text{–}99}\!\big(\log(1 + \max(\text{cond\_surf}_f, 0))\big)$$

### 3.2 Factor `S` (`submodular_skeleton_dots`: Exact 300 m Triangular-Kernel Submodular Expected-$\mathrm{TP}_w$ Allocation)

Given a continuous predicted probability grid $s$ on `valid` (`dom_c` in holdout, `foot` in full build), masked pixels `known` (`vis_c` in holdout, `labels` in full build), and exact target dot count $M_{\text{target}}$:
1. **Case-control Bayes prior-odds calibration (`King & Zeng 2001`, parameter-free):**
   Given prior odds ratio $\tau = \frac{\pi_{\text{true}} / (1 - \pi_{\text{true}})}{r_{\text{train}} / (1 - r_{\text{train}})}$ computed strictly from the **visible training domain** ($\pi_{\text{true}} = |V_{\text{train}}| / |\Omega_{\text{train}}|$, $r_{\text{train}} = \bar{y}_{\text{train}}$; no test/hidden information), transform $p = \text{clip}(s, 0, 1 - 10^{-6})$ via:
   $$\pi_{\text{cal}}(g) = \frac{\tau\, p(g)}{(1 - p(g)) + \tau\, p(g)}$$
2. **Multi-scale curvilinear fault skeleton & candidate pool:**
   $$R(s) = \big(\text{ridge\_nms}(s, \text{valid}, 1.0) \cup \text{ridge\_nms}(s, \text{valid}, 2.0)\big) \cap \text{valid} \cap (\neg\text{known}) \cap (s > 0)$$
   Restrict active skeleton mass $w(g) = \pi_{\text{cal}}(g) \cdot \mathbb{I}(g \in C_{\text{pool}})$ to the top $K_{\text{pool}} = \text{round}(0.045 \cdot |\text{valid}|)$ pixels of $R(s)$ ranked by $s$ (preventing diffuse background playa ripples from diluting tail selections while giving $1.286\times$ the `0.035` candidate ridge pool for cross-fault reallocation).
3. **Exact lazy-greedy submodular maximization (Minoux 1978):**
   Maintain coverage state $c(g) \in [0, 1]$ (initialized to $0$) over the grid and hard micro-collision distance $d \ge 1.5\text{ px}$. Using the exact 29-pixel 300 m kernel offsets `_OFFS` (`k(d) = max(1 - d/3, 0) > 0` from `gems25.metric`), iteratively select up to $M_{\text{target}}$ pixels $x \in C_{\text{pool}}$ maximizing the incremental expected $\mathrm{TP}_w$ gain:
   $$\Delta F(x \mid S_{t-1}) = \sum_{(dy, dx, k) \in \text{OFFS}} w(x_r + dy,\, x_c + dx)\, \max\!\big(0,\; k - c(x_r + dy,\, x_c + dx)\big)$$
   updating $c(x_r + dy, x_c + dx) \leftarrow \max(c, k)$ and suppressing $d < 1.5\text{ px}$ neighbors after each selection.
