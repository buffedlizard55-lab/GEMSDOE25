## 1. Why GEMSDOE32 `h33-h33-2-b2` scored 0.2778 (OWNER-REPORTED) and whether we can beat it

Recomputed pixel identities (`COMPUTED`, `evidence/h71_lane_extended.json` + prior `evidence/focus_sites_forensics.json`):

`H19-5` (121,131 px, 0.1922) → `dot_thin(., 1.5)` D1.5 (0.2477) → `dot_thin(., 2.4)` D2.8 (44,090 px, 0.2600) → `d(cat)>1` R1 (40,199 px, 0.2708) → **`d(cat)>2` B2 (37,654 px, 0.2778)**. Deleted collar dots carried ~0.001–0.005 credit/px ≪ τ ≈ 0.056. GEMSDOE36 adding 1,200 dots to B2 **lowered** the claim to 0.2750.

**Can a unique file score higher than 0.2778?** In principle yes — but only with **new true-fault pixels H19-5 does not cover** at credit/dot > τ. Cloning B2 is a duplicate-lane stop. This session's three catalogue-free DIRECT detectors are geometrically unique (15.4% 3-px overlap vs B2; Spearman 0.001) but their hide-and-recover HOLDOUT-DTI is ~0.05–0.08 vs H35 ~0.15, because Family E (visible-catalogue geometry) dominates the **catalogue-gap proxy**. Live transfer of independent detectors in the cohort has peaked at OWNER-REPORTED **0.2478** (GEMSDOE70). **No ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED score exists in this repo.** Prompt claims 0.3195 / 0.4413 remain unverified.

Official sources: [DrivenData #306](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/), [GDR 1391](https://gdr.openei.org/submissions/1391), [GeoDAWN 10.5066/P93LGLVQ](https://doi.org/10.5066/P93LGLVQ), [EPSG:32611](https://epsg.io/32611), [Tversky index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tversky_index), [reference solution](https://github.com/drivendataorg/gems-prize-reference-solution).

## 5. Limitations and next work (ranked by P(Win))

1. **Do not spend a slot on H71.** Holdout vs H35 failed honestly.
2. **Family E / H19-5 post-processing is saturated** near 0.2778; beating it requires a 2-D spatial model (U-Net / ConvNeXt on patches with differentiable 300 m Tversky loss) or a high-credit complement that is **not** QFFDB-overlapping SGMC.
3. **H71-2 SURF (0.084)** was stronger than BLIND (0.051) on holdout — range-front 3-field polarity is the better of the two HGPC arms if anyone revisits this physics as an *additive* (not this session: frozen primary was BLIND).
4. Hard-zero SGMC-SCARP remains scientifically interesting but is a proxy contaminant (`IR-25-SGMC-PROXY-CONTAMINATION`).
5. GitHub `gh api` rate-limit blocked `fetch_top_scorers.py`; restored via `git fetch`/`git show` instead. Shared tool should use git as primary.
