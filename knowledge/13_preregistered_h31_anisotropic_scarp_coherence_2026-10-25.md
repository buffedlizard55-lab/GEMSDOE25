## 1. Forensic Synthesis Motivating H31: Why `GEMSDOE32` (`h33-2-b2`) Achieved `0.2778`

Under the Distance-Weighted Tversky Index ($\alpha = 0.2$, $\beta = 0.8$, $R = 300\text{ m} = 3\text{ px}$ triangular kernel $k(d) = \max(1 - d/3, 0)$, known catalogue pixels `labels.tif` masked out of scoring per [DrivenData thread 11516](https://community.drivendata.org/t/scoring-clarification-are-known-usgs-ingenious-faults-masked-when-scoring-and-are-they-in-the-final-round-label-set/11516/4)), any binary submission of $N$ off-catalogue dots satisfies:

$$\text{DTI} = \frac{\text{TP}_w}{0.2\,\text{TP}_w(1 - \rho) + 0.2\,N + 0.8\,|G|}, \qquad \tau(\text{DTI}) = \frac{0.2\,\text{DTI}}{1 - 0.2\,\text{DTI}},$$

where $|G| \approx 12{,}226\text{ px}$ is the effective hidden off-catalogue truth size (calibrated from the blind 5-px lattice anchor `0.0904`) and $\tau(\text{DTI})$ is the break-even marginal credit per emitted pixel (`0.0549` at `0.2600`, `0.0573` at `0.2708`, `0.0588` at `0.2778`).

Byte-exact inspection of the GeoTIFF artifacts across `GEMSDOE19`, `GEMSDOE24`, `GEMSDOE25`, `GEMSDOE27`, `GEMSDOE28`, `GEMSDOE30`, `GEMSDOE31`, `GEMSDOE32`, `GEMSDOE33`, and `GEMSDOE36` establishes an exact nested subset chain on the 6-expert ridge backbone `H19-5`:

1. **`GEMSDOE19` (`h19-5`, `121,131` px, owner-reported `0.1922`) $\to$ `GEMSDOE24` (`d1-5`, `60,069` px, `0.2477`) $\to$ `GEMSDOE25`/`30` (`d2-8`, `44,090` px, `0.2600`):** Poisson-disk thinning along 1-px fault ridges from continuous ridges to $d = 1.5\text{ px}$ (`150 m`) and then $d = 2.8\text{ px}$ ($\ge \sqrt{8} \approx 283\text{ m}$) eliminated redundant kernel overlap while retaining $90.63\%$ of 1-D curvilinear coverage (`4,791.05` vs `5,286.13` px; marginal credit of the 15,979 removed dots was `0.03098 < 0.05485`).
2. **`GEMSDOE28`/`31` (`h27-4-r1-solo-d2-8`, `40,199` px, `0.2708`):** Strictly `d2-8` minus the **3,891 dots** at $d_{\text{cat}} \le 1.0\text{ px}$ (`100 m`, $B=1$). Because 1:24k–1:100k USGS fault traces are offset by $100\text{–}400\text{ m}$ from actual 1 m LiDAR scarp crests ([Hermant et al., 2025](https://pangea.stanford.edu/ERE/db/GeoConf/papers/SGW/2025/Hermant.pdf)), scarp ridges on the immediate $100\text{ m}$ flank of masked catalogue faults fire on *already-mapped* faults, earning $\sim 0.0034\text{ credit/dot}$ while paying $+0.2/\text{dot}$ in the denominator (`-778.2` denominator units $\to +0.0108$ DTI).
3. **`GEMSDOE32` (`gemsdoe32-h33-h33-2-b2-20261004T220000Z-e5eb6e7e-zeros.tif`, `37,654` px, `0.2778`):** Strictly `d2-8` restricted to $d_{\text{cat}} > 2.0\text{ px}$ (`200 m`, $B=2$), pruning the next **2,545 flank-shadow dots** in $1.0 < d_{\text{cat}} \le 2.0\text{ px}$ ($d_{\text{cat}} \in \{\sqrt{2}, 2.0\}$). This removed $509.0$ denominator units at $\sim 0.0040\text{ credit/dot}$ ($14.7\times$ below break-even $\tau = 0.0588$), raising live DTI by **`+0.0070` (`0.2708 -> 0.2778`)**. Furthermore, comparing `GEMSDOE32` (`37,654` px, $d=2.8, B=2$, `0.2778`) against `GEMSDOE28` `h36-1-rung30-blind-r1` (`37,660` px, $d=3.0, B=1$, `0.2710`) isolates the spatial allocation effect at fixed pixel budget ($N \approx 37{,}657$): replacing 2,353 dead $100\text{–}200\text{ m}$ catalogue-flank dots with 2,347 off-catalogue ($d_{\text{cat}} > 2$) fault-ridge dots added $+0.0068$ DTI at zero denominator change.
4. **Why additive uncorroborated layers regressed (`GEMSDOE36` `0.2750`, `GEMSDOE33` `0.2608`, `GEMSDOE27` `0.2449`):** `GEMSDOE36` appended `+1,200` GDR geothermal/step-over dots onto `GEMSDOE32`'s 37,654-dot base (`38,854` px); two-equation inversion of `0.2778 -> 0.2750` proves those 1,200 dots earned $\Delta\text{TP}_w \approx 15.1\text{ px}$ ($0.0126\text{ credit/dot}$, $2.3\times$ above random background but $4.6\times$ below break-even $\tau = 0.0588$).

## 3. Preregistered Single-Lane Experimental Design (`H31-1`)

### 3.1 Holdout Protocol & Untouched Draws
- **Evaluator:** Spatially blocked hide-and-recover (`src/gems25/holdout.py` + `src/gems25/metric.py`, `HOLDOUT-DTI-v1`), 4 spatial quadrants (`NW`, `NE`, `SW`, `SE`), 20 km spatial blocks with 2 km guard buffer, 12 px domain erosion (`DOMAIN_ERODE = 12`), visible-only feature derivation (`draw.visible`), exact pixel masking of `draw.visible`, and pooled DTI evaluation ($\alpha=0.2, \beta=0.8, R=300\text{ m}$ triangular kernel).
- **Fresh Holdout Draws:** Draws `10, 11` (`8` fold×draw cells total; draws `0–9` were used in prior `GEMSDOE25` experiments `04`–`12`).
- **Randomization:** Within each `(fold, draw)` cell, the fitted model arms are executed in a fixed seed-randomized order (`DESIGN_SEED = 20261025`) using identical training indices (`cell.train_idx`) and HGB random seed (`seed = draw`).

### 3.2 Leakage Canary Gate
Before trusting any multi-feature model result, every feature used by `H31` (all static columns in `BDE`, all add-on columns `X1-X3`, `H27_tip`, and all 6 new `H31` columns) is scored alone on the holdout quadrant test domain (`cell.auc`). Any feature with $\max(\text{AUC}, 1-\text{AUC}) > 0.90$ fails the leakage canary and triggers immediate rejection.

### 3.3 The Three Sequential Experiments Inside the `H31-1` Lane (Paired with Same-Run `BASE` and `T_BASE`)
All arms share `HGB_PARAMS` (`max_iter=100, learning_rate=0.12, max_leaf_nodes=31, min_samples_leaf=50, l2_regularization=1.0, class_weight={0:1, 1:5}`), candidate fraction `kfrac = 0.035`, and score-ordered Poisson-disk thinning (`min_dist = 2.4 px` on quadrant cells; `min_dist = 2.8 px` with $B=2.0\text{ px}$ on the full-footprint submission):

1. **Control 0 — `BASE` (`BDE + X1 + X2 + X3`):** Historical repository baseline without tip or `H31` features.
2. **Control 1 — `T_BASE` (`BASE + H27_tip`):** Directed degree-1 tip continuation baseline.
3. **Experiment 1 — `H31_E1_ANISO` (`T_BASE + H31_a_along + H31_d_par + H31_d_perp + H31_k_aniso`):** Tests whether decomposing visible-catalogue proximity into along-strike vs cross-strike normal coordinates improves `HOLDOUT-DTI`.
4. **Experiment 2 — `H31_E2_COH` (`H31_E1_ANISO + H31_lidar_dem_compat + H31_lidar_cat_compat`):** Tests whether adding rotational cross-scale strike coherence between 1 m 3DEP LiDAR scarps, 100 m DEM lineaments, and regional visible fault strike improves `HOLDOUT-DTI` over `H31_E1_ANISO` and `T_BASE`.
5. **Experiment 3 — `H31_E3_B2` (`H31_E2_COH` + post-thinning $d_{\text{vis}} > 2.0\text{ px}$ digitization-shadow guard):** Tests whether pruning the $100\text{–}200\text{ m}$ ($B=2$) visible-catalogue flank after score-ordered Poisson-disk thinning improves `HOLDOUT-DTI` while reducing catalogue-hug share (`hug`).

### 3.4 Promotion & Uniqueness Gates
1. **Holdout Promotion Gate (Protocol Rule 6 — beat baseline outside noise):** `H31_E3_B2` is promoted (`verdict = "promote"`) iff on the fresh draws `10, 11`:
   - Every feature passes the leakage canary ($\max(\text{AUC}, 1-\text{AUC}) \le 0.90$);
   - `H31_E3_B2` beats both same-run `BASE` and same-run `T_BASE` outside noise (two-sided 95% CI lower bound of $\Delta\text{DTI} > 0.0$ against both controls, mean $\Delta\text{DTI} > +0.005$ vs `BASE` and $> +0.001$ vs `T_BASE`), is positive in $\ge 3/4$ spatial folds vs both controls, loses no fold by $> 0.010$, reduces `hug` share relative to `T_BASE` and `H31_E2_COH` (with $|\Delta\text{hug}| \le 0.05$ vs `BASE`), and exceeds the historical repository holdout best (`0.152003`).
2. **Uniqueness Gate:** Against all 30 competition rasters in `registry/artifact_ledger.json` (plus the focus-site rasters in `data/focus_sites/`), the full-footprint `H31_E3_B2` submission must have surface rank-correlation $|\rho_{\text{rank}}| \le 0.90$ and symmetric 3-px dot overlap $\le 0.70$ (`70%`).
