### 1.2 Exact Distance-Tolerant Tversky (`DTI`) Calculus & Break-Even Economics

On the `5,167,373`-pixel valid Great Basin footprint $\Omega$ (`3730 × 3292` grid at `100 m` resolution in `EPSG:32611`) with `60,988` public Quaternary fault pixels $C$ (`labels.tif`) masked from scoring and unknown hidden fault pixels $G \subset \Omega \setminus C$, let $K(d) = \max(0, 1 - d/R)$ with $R = 3.0\text{ px}$ (`300 m`) be the linear tent kernel, $\tilde{p}(x) = \max_{z} \hat{y}(z) K(\|x - z\|_2)$, and $\tilde{y}(x) = \max_{g \in G} K(\|x - g\|_2)$. With $\alpha = 0.2$ and $\beta = 0.8$:
$$\mathrm{DTI}(\hat{y}, G) = \frac{\mathrm{TP}_w}{\mathrm{TP}_w + 0.2\,\mathrm{FP}_w + 0.8\,\mathrm{FN}_w}, \qquad \mathrm{TP}_w + \mathrm{FN}_w = Y = \sum_{x \in \Omega \setminus C} \tilde{y}(x).$$

Differentiating $\mathrm{DTI}$ with respect to adding or deleting a small subset of unit dots of total buffered mass $\Delta M = \Delta \mathrm{TP}_w + \Delta \mathrm{FP}_w$ around an operating point $\mathrm{DTI}_0$ yields the exact **marginal credit per unit buffered mass break-even threshold** $\tau(\mathrm{DTI}_0)$:
$$\Delta \mathrm{DTI} > 0 \iff \frac{\Delta \mathrm{TP}_w}{\Delta M} > \tau(\mathrm{DTI}_0) = \frac{\alpha\,\mathrm{DTI}_0}{1 - \alpha\,\mathrm{DTI}_0 + \alpha\,\mathrm{DTI}_0} \approx \frac{0.2\,\mathrm{DTI}_0}{1 - 0.2\,\mathrm{DTI}_0}\text{ (per } \Delta\mathrm{FP}_w\text{), or } \frac{\Delta \mathrm{TP}_w}{\Delta M} > \frac{0.2\,\mathrm{DTI}_0}{1 + 0.8\,\mathrm{DTI}_0}\text{ (under } \mathrm{FN}_w = N_{\text{hid}} - \mathrm{TP}_w\text{)}.$$
Under the repository's convention (`scripts/analyze_focus_sites.py`, `N_hidden ≈ 12,691` px), break-even credit per emitted dot at $\mathrm{DTI}_0 \in [0.260, 0.278]$ is **$\tau \in [0.0549, 0.0588]$**, whereas `B2`'s `37,654` retained dots average **`0.1319` true-fault credit/dot**.
