### 1.1 Exact Metric Calculus Behind the Focus-Site Progression

The official GEMS evaluation metric (`src/gems25/metric.py`, `dti_binary`) evaluates predictions $p \in [0, 1]^{\Omega}$ on valid footprint pixels $\Omega$ (`5,167,373` pixels at `100 m` posting in `EPSG:32611`) after pixel-exact masking of known catalogue pixels $K$ (`labels.tif`, `60,988` pixels inside $\Omega$), using a 300 m ($R = 3.0\text{ px}$) radial triangular tent kernel $K_R(\Delta r, \Delta c) = \max\bigl(0,\, 1 - \sqrt{\Delta r^2 + \Delta c^2}/3.0\bigr)$:

$$\text{DTI} = \frac{\text{TP}}{\text{TP} + \alpha\,\text{FP} + \beta\,\text{FN}}, \qquad \alpha = 0.2,\quad \beta = 0.8$$

where $\tilde{p}(y) = \max_{x \in \Omega \setminus K} p(x)\,K_R(x, y)$ and $\tilde{t}(y) = \max_{x \in T_{\text{hid}}} K_R(x, y)$ are the 300 m tent-dilated prediction and hidden-truth surfaces on $\Omega \setminus K$, with $\text{TP} = \sum \min(\tilde{p}, \tilde{t})$, $\text{FP} = \sum \max(\tilde{p} - \tilde{t}, 0)$, and $\text{FN} = \sum \max(\tilde{t} - \tilde{p}, 0)$. Note the official reference notebook (`drivendataorg/gems-prize-reference-solution`, `unet-mc-cv-reference-solution.ipynb` by Prof. John Lipor) trains a Monte-Carlo cross-validated U-Net directly against pixel binary cross-entropy without accounting for asymmetric tent dilation ($R = 3.0\text{ px}$, $\alpha = 0.2 \ll \beta = 0.8$).

Differentiating $\text{DTI}$ with respect to adding one unit-mass binary dot ($p(x) = 1$) that contributes $\Delta\text{TP}$ of new tent-covered truth overlap (hence $1 - \Delta\text{TP}$ of false-positive mass on the point-mass emission scale before kernel overlap saturation, or $\Delta M_{\text{dil}} - \Delta\text{TP}$ on the dilated surface) yields the exact **marginal break-even inclusion ratio** (`gems25.metric.marginal_inclusion_ratio`):

$$\frac{\Delta\text{TP}}{\Delta M} > \tau(D) = \frac{\alpha\,D}{1 - \alpha\,D}$$

At $D = 0.2600$, $\tau = 0.05485$; at $D = 0.2708$, $\tau = 0.05726$; at $D = 0.2778$, $\tau = 0.05883$ (`COMPUTED`). Every emitted dot earning less than $\tau(D)$ of uncovered hidden-fault overlap **strictly lowers** $\text{DTI}$ via the $0.2\,\text{FP}$ denominator tax.

### 3.1 Exact Formulas for the 7 Static `H36` Sidecar Columns (`src/gems25/h36.py`)

All 7 static columns are computed strictly from `data/work/bands/` and `data/external/` (`geodawn_extensions_u8.tif`, `lidar_scarp_features_u8.tif`, `gdr_wellspring_in_footprint.csv`) with **zero access to `labels.tif` or `existing_faults.tif`**, stored in gitignored `data/work/h36_columns.npy` of shape `(7, 5_167_373)` `float32`:

1. **`H36_grav_spi_k`** — Unit-consistent gravity SPI local wavenumber:
   Smooth `iso_grav_anom` ($g$, mGal) and `iso_grav_anom_vg` ($g_z$, mGal/km) with anti-aliasing Gaussian $\sigma = 1.0\text{ px}$; compute isotropic horizontal gradient $(g_x, g_y) = \nabla g / 0.1\text{ km}$ (`mGal/km`), $H_g = \sqrt{g_x^2 + g_y^2}$, unit-consistent tilt $\theta_g = \arctan2(g_z, H_g + 0.05)$, and local wavenumber $\kappa_g = \log\bigl(1 + \|\nabla\theta_g / 0.1\text{ km}\|\bigr)$.
2. **`H36_mag_spi_k`** — Unit-consistent magnetic SPI local wavenumber:
   Smooth `tmi` ($T$, nT) and unit-converted vertical gradient $T_z = 1000.0 \cdot \text{tmi\_vg}$ (`nT/km`) with $\sigma = 1.0\text{ px}$; compute $(T_x, T_y) = \nabla T / 0.1\text{ km}$ (`nT/km`), $H_T = \sqrt{T_x^2 + T_y^2}$, unit-consistent tilt $\theta_m = \arctan2(T_z, H_T + 2.0)$, and local wavenumber $\kappa_m = \log\bigl(1 + \|\nabla\theta_m / 0.1\text{ km}\|\bigr)$.
3. **`H36_up150_persist`** — GeoDAWN `TMI_up150` upward-continuation vertical persistence:

   $\frac{\text{geod\_dilaterate}}{\text{geod\_2ndinv} + 1.0} \cdot \log\Bigl(1 + \frac{\text{ieq\_n100a15}}{\text{deq\_n100a15} + 100.0}\Bigr)$.
6. **`H36_chem_diseq`** — GDR hydrogeochemical geothermometer disequilibrium (`27,092` wells/springs):
   For each in-footprint row of `gdr_wellspring_in_footprint.csv` with finite chemical geothermometer $T_{\text{chem}} = \max(\text{geothermquartz\_c}, \text{geothermchalc\_c}, \text{geothermcat\_c})$, compute excess reservoir disequilibrium $\Delta T = \text{clip}\bigl(T_{\text{chem}} - \max(\text{temp\_c}, 15.0),\, 0,\, 250\bigr)$ (capturing cooled/mixed blind-system outflows), accumulate pixel maximum, smooth with $\sigma = 25\text{ px}$ (`2.5 km` hydrothermal plume dispersion scale), and take $\log(1 + \Delta T_{\sigma=25})$.
7. **`H36_burial_regime`** — Sedimentary burial & 3DEP LiDAR-void subsurface exposure weight:
   Let $u_{\text{flat}}(x) = 1 - \text{rank\_pct}_{\text{foot}}(\text{det\_elev\_slope})(x) \in [0, 1]$ and $v_{\text{void}}(x) = \mathbb{I}[L_{\text{valid}}(x) == 0]$. Define $\text{H36\_burial\_regime}(x) = \bigl(0.65\,u_{\text{flat}}(x) + 0.35\,v_{\text{void}}(x)\bigr)\,\sqrt{\kappa_g(x)\cdot\kappa_m(x)}$.

### 3.2 Out-of-Fold Dual-Head Product-of-Experts (`PoE`) & Exact 300 m Submodular Emission (`SUBMOD-300`)

Inside each `(fold, draw)` cell of `gems25.experiment.Cell(ctx, fold, draw, extras=True, h27=True)` (`HGB_PARAMS` unchanged from `src/gems25/experiment.py`, `random_state = draw`):

- **`Head S` (Zero-Topography Crustal/Subsurface Head — `53` cols):** `ctx.columns("ACDE")` (`45` cols, **zero `Family B` DEM or 3DEP LiDAR**) + `H27_tip` (`1` col, computed strictly from `draw.visible`) + all `7` `H36` columns.
- **`Head M` (Integrated Multi-Physics Head — `84` cols):** `ctx.columns("ABCDE") + ctx.named(EXTRAS)` (`76` cols) + `H27_tip` (`1` col) + all `7` `H36` columns.
- **Regime-Gated Product-of-Experts Surface (`s_PoE`):**
  With subsurface regime exponent $\alpha_{\text{sub}}(x) = \text{clip}\bigl(0.35 + 0.35\,u_{\text{flat}}(x) + 0.15\,v_{\text{void}}(x),\; 0.35,\; 0.85\bigr)$:
  $$s_{\text{PoE}}(x) = p_M(x)^{1 - \alpha_{\text{sub}}(x)} \, p_S(x)^{\alpha_{\text{sub}}(x)}$$
- **Elkan (2001) Prior-Shift Calibration + Exact 300 m Tent Submodular Allocation (`submod300_emit`):**
  1. Convert raw subsampled odds $\frac{s(y)}{1 - s(y)}$ (trained at positive fraction $\pi_{\text{tr}} = \bar{y}_{\text{train}}$) to spatial hidden-fault prevalence $\pi_0 = 0.0024$:
     $$\hat{\pi}(y) = \frac{r_{\text{prior}} \, s(y)}{(1 - s(y)) + r_{\text{prior}} \, s(y)}, \qquad r_{\text{prior}} = \frac{\pi_0 / (1 - \pi_0)}{\pi_{\text{tr}} / (1 - \pi_{\text{tr}})}$$
  2. Apply **fold-safe tip-preserved lateral-flank shielding** (`IR-25-GEMSDOE32-B2-PARADOX`): zero out non-tip lateral flanks within $B = 2.0\text{ px}$ of **visible** faults only (`shield = (d(y, visible) <= 2.0) & (tip_vis(y) < 0.06)`), defining expected hidden-truth weight $w(y) = \hat{\pi}(y)$ on `domain & ~visible & ~shield` and $0$ elsewhere.
  3. Restrict candidate dot centers to the centered dual-regime skeleton `cand = domain & ~visible & ~shield & (ridge_nms(s, 1.0) | ridge_nms(p_S, 1.2))` (top `3.5%` of domain by $s$), and select up to $M = \text{round}(m_{\text{frac}} \cdot |\text{domain}|)$ binary dots ($m_{\text{frac}} = 37{,}654 / 5{,}167{,}373 = 0.00728687$, stopping early only if marginal uncovered gain drops below $\tau_{\min} = 10^{-6}$) by **exact 1-by-1 Minoux (1978) lazy-accelerated greedy maximization** of $F(X) = \sum_y w(y)\max_{x \in X} K_{3.0}(x, y)$.

### 3.3 Paired Holdout Arms, Untouched Draws, and Promotion Gate

- **Evaluator:** `gems25.metric.dti_binary` (`alpha = 0.2, beta = 0.8, R = 3.0 px`) via `gems25.experiment.Cell.evaluate` (`gems25-holdout-v1`, `hide_frac = 0.20`, `15 px = 1.5 km` collar, `DOMAIN_ERODE = 12`).
- **Experiment 1 (`E1` Screen):** Folds `0, 1, 2, 3` (`NW, NE, SW, SE`) $\times$ untouched draws `20, 21` (`8` cells).
  Paired arms evaluated in every cell:
  1. `C0_BDE_ctrl`: Same-run incumbent comparator (`BDE + 7 addons`, `sapd2.4` at `kfrac = 0.035, d = 2.4`).
  2. `C1_Sub_ctrl`: Same-run zero-topography baseline (`ACDE + H27_tip` without `H36` columns, `sapd2.4` at `kfrac = 0.035, d = 2.4`).
  3. `H36_S_submod`: Pure zero-topography crustal head (`Head S`: `ACDE + H27_tip + 7 H36 cols`) + `submod300_emit`.
  4. `H36_PoE_submod` (**PRIMARY**): Regime-gated Product-of-Experts (`Head M × Head S`) + `submod300_emit`.
  5. `H36_PoE_sapd` (diagnostic emission ablation, no separate gate): Same `s_PoE` surface emitted with standard `sapd2.4` (`kfrac = 0.035, d = 2.4`) to isolate the exact contribution of `submod300_emit` vs `H36` features/PoE.

  Standalone out-of-fold ROC-AUC (`max(AUC, 1 - AUC)`) on `Cell` hidden-test positives vs `150,000` domain negatives for all `7` `H36` columns + `H27_tip`. **Hard stop:** if any feature exceeds `0.90`, abort immediately as leakage.
- **Experiment 2 (`E2` Confirmation):** Folds `0, 1, 2, 3` $\times$ untouched draws `22, 23` (`8` fresh cells), run with **zero parameter changes** on `C0_BDE_ctrl`, `C1_Sub_ctrl`, `H36_S_submod`, and `H36_PoE_submod`.
