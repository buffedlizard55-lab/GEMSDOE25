### Why the reported GEMSDOE32 B2 result may be higher

The pinned pixel forensics describe a nested mask lineage: D2.8 has 44,090 positive cells; H27-4 has 40,199; B2 has 37,654. B2 is a subset of H27-4, which is a subset of D2.8. The progression removes 6,436 cells within two pixels of the known catalogue; the last pruning step removes 2,545 cells. Under an asymmetric detection objective, removing low-credit predictions near already mapped faults can reduce false-positive mass more than it reduces credit. That is a **plausible metric mechanism**, not proof that the deleted predictions were false positives on hidden truth, not proof of causality, and not a forecast that a new candidate will score better. The supplied 0.2778 itself is an owner claim, not an organizer receipt.
