### Selection and frozen test

CBEC is the sole candidate selected for the first screen because it can be formed from existing cached bands and addresses a distinct cross-layer mechanism. Use `src/gems25/experiment.py` / `src/gems25/holdout.py` and the existing checker/writer code; do not build a private copy of the holdout metric. The test is a paired 2×2 factorial (conductivity/base edge factor absent/present × gravity-edge concordance factor absent/present), with the same folds, fresh draws 116 and 117, train/test partition, negative sampling, HGB settings, candidate budget, thinning, and emitter for all four arms. Recompute the H35 incumbent in every cell. The fixed primary candidate is the combined arm; factorial component arms are diagnostics, not alternative winner selection. The catalogue features `E_*` must be rebuilt from `draw.visible` only; `draw.visible` is masked pixel-exactly in scoring; whole connected catalogue components are withheld with the evaluator's spatial-block buffer.

The fixed promotion-to-next-experiment screen (not to a submission slot) requires the combined arm to improve paired pooled HOLDOUT-DTI over same-run H35 by at least `+0.002`, a quadrant-cluster 95% paired CI lower bound above zero, positive gain in at least 3 of 4 folds, worst fold gain no less than `-0.010`, and all feature canaries at or below 0.90. Failure means negative; no confirmation or raster build.

Before fitting: verify pinned caches and library versions; run each new CBEC column alone through the existing finite-value and missingness-aware leakage canary (effective AUC above 0.90 is a stop); record the exact evaluator/source hashes and withheld-positive count. Primary response: pooled DTI with alpha 0.2, beta 0.8, and the 300 m triangular kernel, reported as **HOLDOUT-DTI** with evaluator version, number of withheld-positive occurrences, and quadrant-cluster 95% CI. A gain must beat the same-run incumbent under the predeclared paired spatial-fold rule; a HOLDOUT-DTI value is not a leaderboard score or projection.

No candidate raster is permitted in this preregistration. If the experiment passes, the later surface and final dots must each pass the literal registry checks (absolute rank correlation <=0.90 and <=70% of candidate dots within 3 px of every registry raster); a dense-raster caveat does not waive the protocol. The exact written raster must then be checked with the shared submission validator for the organizer template's CRS, shape, transform, single band, finite footprint values in [0,1], and byte hash. Failure at any gate is a negative result and stops publication. The weekly-slot selector remains separate.
