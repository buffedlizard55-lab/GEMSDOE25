**Scientific grounding (READ, not a claim about the hidden test labels).** Faulds & Hinz report in a 2015 Great Basin inventory that step-overs/relay ramps, fault terminations, and fault intersections are common geothermal settings, while major range-front faults are comparatively uncommon: [OSTI-hosted paper](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/1724082). This motivates H27-1, but it does **not** establish that the competition's new fault labels have the same distribution. The labels score faults, not geothermal vents. The interpretation of fault-tip continuation is an inference to be tested.

## H27-1 pre-registration

### Primary question
Does a catalogue-tip / relay-gap feature, and does its interaction with an independent topographic scarp, improve spatially blocked hide-and-recover DTI over the repository's current same-harness incumbent?

### Factors and 2² design

- **T (directed tip continuation, −1/+1):** off/on. Find degree-1 endpoints in the 8-neighbour graph of the *visible* catalogue; omit isolated pixels. At each endpoint with full 3×3 footprint support, estimate line orientation from the same 2-px-smoothed doubled-angle structure tensor used by family E; use family E's 20-px Gaussian resultant-length coherence for the local visible trace. Estimate outward direction as endpoint minus its sole visible 8-neighbour. For each candidate, use its nearest endpoint. Set T=0 for endpoint distance `<1.5` or `>12` px, local orientation coherence `<0.25`, line-parallel alignment `cos(2Δθ)<0.5`, or outward-direction dot product `<0.5`. Otherwise `T = exp(−d/5) × ((cos(2Δθ)−0.5)/0.5) × ((outward_dot−0.5)/0.5) × coherence`, clipped to [0,1]. This is a raster-derived endpoint proxy, not a surveyed vector endpoint.
- **S (cross-scale scarp support, −1/+1):** off/on. `S = scale01(L_step_max) × max(scale01(B_crest), scale01(B_trough))`, where each `scale01` is fixed at the 1st and 99th percentiles from the existing training-footprint sample and clipped to [0,1]. This asks whether the high-resolution 3DEP-derived step and broader 100 m DEM lineament agree.
- Fit the four fixed configurations: base only; base+T; base+S; base+T+S+T×S, with `T×S` the product column. This full 2² design estimates T, S, and T×S response contrasts; no interaction is inferred from an OFAT pair.
- **Fixed base:** the existing `BDE + X1 + X2 + X3` model (`X1_K/ThK/UK`, `X2_compat/coh`, `X3_gm/gd`), with every setting unchanged. For every cell use the existing score-ordered Poisson-disk emitter at 2.4 px, top 3.5% candidate budget, from `evidence/addons/emk_extension.json`. Refit this incumbent in every new cell so comparisons are paired.

### Holdout, execution, analysis, and gate

- Use the existing four spatial quadrants (NW, NE, SW, SE), common random numbers, 1.5 km collar, visible-only catalogue features, and the existing hide-and-recover protocol. Previously analyzed draws 0–3 are excluded from this new screen: **screen draws 4,5**; only if the frozen gate passes, run a **confirmation on fresh draws 6,7**. These are new random component hides, not independent geographies; four spatial blocks remain the effective spatial replication.
- Randomize the four design-row order with seed `20261003`; save the exact matrix and order before running. Primary response is exact sparse DTI after the fixed emission. Report emitted pixels, credit coverage, AUC, and catalogue-hug share as diagnostics.
- Estimate main effects and the T×S interaction by the 2² contrasts, per spatial fold (mean over the two draws) and pooled; preserve all cell rows. No new hyperparameter search, threshold selection, or post-hoc reranking is allowed.
- The historical comparator is **0.152003389** (`evidence/addons/emk_extension.json`; draws 0,1), repository proxy evidence only. **Promotion requires both** (a) the combined T+S arm exceeds 0.152003389 on the new screen, and (b) versus the same-draw paired base it gains >0.001 mean DTI, is positive in ≥3/4 folds, loses no fold by more than 0.01, and increases catalogue-hug share by no more than 0.10. Apply the same two gates again to draws 6,7 before any candidate is eligible for a weekly slot. A proxy pass is necessary, not sufficient; no live score is inferred.
- If the screen fails, stop the H27-1 experiment, record the negative result, and do not package a H27-1 TIFF. If it passes, confirmation is mandatory before packaging. Uploading remains manual; this repository does not access DrivenData submission pages.

## Verification and limitations

- Official pages checked for this register: [Faulds & Hinz 2015, OSTI](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/1724082); [GDR 1391](https://gdr.openei.org/submissions/1391); [USGS 3DEP](https://www.usgs.gov/3d-elevation-program); [USGS ScienceBase heat-flow release](https://www.sciencebase.gov/catalog/item/6297d2fad34ec53d276c5b28).
- GDR 1391 describes itself as publicly accessible under CC BY 4.0 and lists the 2 m probe and paleo-feature downloads. Their file pages were reachable through the official HTML fetch, but direct binary retrieval from this sandbox failed. No claim is made that their data were downloaded or validated.
- No alternative idea is submitted merely because it is geologically plausible. In particular, H27-2/3/4 are blocked pending the source/coverage checks above; H27-1 is the only candidate in this register that can be tested with locally restored layers.

## H27-1 screen outcome (post-run addendum, 2026-10-02)

The frozen screen ran on draws 4,5 in all four spatial folds with the randomized four-row order recorded in `evidence/h27_screen/design.json`; all 32 cells are retained in `evidence/h27_screen/cells.jsonl` and the summary is in `evidence/h27_screen/results.md`/`results.json`.

Estimated coded contrasts: T **+0.008120** (positive in 4/4 folds), S **−0.000196**, and T×S **−0.001622**. The registered combined TS arm improved over its same-draw base by +0.007924 (4/4 folds; hug-share +0.0123), passing that paired criterion, but its mean DTI **0.151064** did **not** exceed the historical comparator **0.152003389**. The frozen absolute gate therefore **fails**. Per preregistration, the confirmation on draws 6,7 was not run, no H27-1 TIFF was packaged, and no weekly slot was used. The T-only arm's screen mean (0.152882) is a factor contrast, not a registered promotion decision; it is unconfirmed and is not slot-eligible. Any follow-up T-only candidate would require a new preregistration and fresh validation, not a post-hoc promotion.

**Interpretation.** Directed tip geometry has a positive signal in this catalogue-gap proxy, while the cross-scale scarp term adds essentially no main effect and its interaction is slightly negative. Because the designated combined candidate failed the strict absolute holdout gate, this experiment does not justify a competition upload. The catalogue-hide proxy remains an imperfect stand-in for new faults.
