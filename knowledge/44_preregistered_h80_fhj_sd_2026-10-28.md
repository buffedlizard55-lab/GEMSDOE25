## 2. Exact Mathematical Specification of Selected Implementation (`H80`: `H80-1` + `H80-2` + `H80-3`)

We implement the top three local-data hypotheses (`H80-1`, `H80-2`, `H80-3`) jointly as a 6-column strictly static, label-free `float32` feature sidecar `H80_FHJ_NAMES` in `src/gems25/h80_fhj.py`:

1. `H80_bimodal_junction_det`: Förstner structure-tensor junction invariant $\frac{4\det J_\sigma}{\operatorname{tr} J_\sigma + \varepsilon}$ ($\sigma_{\text{grad}}=1.8\text{ px}, \sigma_{\text{ten}}=5.0\text{ px}$) fused across `iso_grav_anom`, destriped `rtp`, and $\log(1+\text{depth\_to\_base\_surf})$, gated by positive transtensional geodetic strain (`0.30 + 0.70 * pct(geod_shearrate + max(0, geod_dilaterate))`).
2. `H80_relay_tip_divergence`: Along-strike directional derivative $|\mathbf{t}\cdot\nabla A_{\text{pot}}|$ multiplied by 8-px doubled-strike dispersion $(1 - \|\overline{\mathbf{n}}_2\|_{\sigma=8\text{ px}})$ and microseismic swarm factor `0.30 + 0.70 * pct(log1p(deq_n100a15) + log1p(ieq_n100a15))`.
3. `H80_cation_silica_diseq`: Label-free GDR #1391 disequilibrium impulse $\Delta T_{\text{qtz}-\text{meas}} + 0.7\Delta T_{\text{cat}-\text{chalc}} + 0.5(T_{\text{meas}}-20)_+$ plus Quaternary volcanic vents (`+25` eq. units), diffused anisotropically ($\sigma_{\parallel}=14\text{ px}, \sigma_{\perp}=3.5\text{ px}$) along Great Basin normal (`015°`) and Walker Lane (`145°`) azimuths, coupled with flanking MT clay-cap gradient $|\nabla\log(1+\text{cond\_surf})|$.
4. `H80_cospectral_theta_basin`: Burial-depth-invariant Cooper-Cowan $\cos\Theta_{\text{grav}} \cdot \cos\Theta_{\text{mag,ds}} \cdot \cos^2(\phi_{\text{grav}} - \phi_{\text{mag}})$ ridge weighted by alluvial basin cover `0.25 + 0.75 * pct(log1p(depth_to_base_surf))`.
5. `H80_aitchison_clr_jump`: Moisture-invariant Aitchison $\operatorname{CLR}(K,Th,U)$ cross-strike jump $\|\mathbf{n}_{\text{pot}}\cdot\nabla\operatorname{CLR}(K,Th,U)_{\sigma=1.8}\|_2 \cdot \exp(-|\mathbf{n}_{\text{pot}}\cdot\nabla\ln TC_{\sigma=1.8}| / \tau_{TC})$ on valid GeoDAWN radiometric pixels (`0` where `K,Th,U == 0`).
6. `H80_sluth_fault_index`: 2-D FFT upward continuation of destriped `rtp` to $z_1=150\text{ m}$ and $z_2=400\text{ m}$ ($z_{\text{ref}}=150\text{ m}$), local analytic-signal decay exponent $\eta_{\text{local}} = \frac{\ln(A_{150}/(A_{400}+\varepsilon))}{\ln((400+z_{\text{ref}})/(150+z_{\text{ref}}))}$, structural-index filter $\exp(-(\eta_{\text{local}}-1.0)^2/0.50^2) \cdot \text{pct}(\ln(1+A_{150}))$.
