## What we tested / did not prove

- Hypothesis: buried magnetic contact beneath comparatively uniform putative radiometric cover could indicate a blind fault missing from the mapped catalogue. A buried intrusive contact or flight-line artifact could mimic the signal. Formula: sigma=3-px gradients of RTP (band 2) divided by one plus normalized gradient of `tc` (band 6). **Physical identification disputed:** the owner-mirrored TIFF tags describe `tc` as magnetic tilt/curvature, whereas the prior numerical audit reports Spearman 0.999866 to a GeoDAWN radiometric total-count layer ([band audit](../evidence/band_semantics.json)). This is not a confirmed geothermal-vent detector. We did not verify an external USGS page in this sandbox.
- The three preregistered experiments, all in one method lane: E1 stopped before placement because a pinned historical raster has an off-grid origin; E2 checked aligned references but stopped because only 2,239 of the required 2,537 R2 pixels were >3 px from the catalogue; E3 emitted a new 8,137-dot raster under a prespecified feasible allocation. **Budget exhausted; no further experiments.**
