## 1. Pre-Fit Forensic Diagnosis Motivating H72

Three byte-verified findings from the 9-site portfolio and `src/gems25/` audit govern this design:

1. **Catalogue-Apron Propensity Collapse (`IR-25-CATALOGUE-HALO-CONVERGENCE`)**:
   - In `Holdout` (`20%` component withholding on `labels.tif`), `47.6%` of withheld test fault pixels sit within `2–9 px` (`200–900 m`) of `d.visible` (`19.4%` at `> 20 px`, median `9.1 px`), whereas `55.1%` of footprint background pixels sit at `> 20 px` (`17.1%` in `2–9 px`, median `23.1 px`).
   - Uniform negative sampling (`N_NEG = 300,000`) plus isotropic Family `E` features (`E_dist`, `E_dens5`, `E_dens20`, `E_dens50`) induces an `~8.0x` Bayesian prior tilt toward the `2–9 px` apron (`2.78 / 0.35`). Consequently, every `GEMSDOE25` Family-`E` HGB raster (`H31`, `H35`, `H36-SPIGM`, `H38`, `H66`) concentrated `68.7%–71.4%` of its dots inside `2–9 px` (`2.0%–3.0%` at `> 20 px`), producing:
     - `78.8%` 3-px dot overlap with external `pindrop-nodes.tif` and `83.5%–97.4%` overlap within `own_lineage` (failing the 70% lane rule even outside `r13-lattice-s5`), and
     - live DrivenData apron collapse on `H66` (`0.0742` `OWNER-REPORTED`), whereas every live scorer `>= 0.219` (`G32 B2 = 0.2778`, `G70 OOF38k = 0.2478`, `G49 ORTHO = 0.2376`, `G27 H29-S = 0.2190`) places `40.9%–49.2%` of dots at `> 20 px` and `23.4%–35.8%` in `2–9 px`.
   - Conversely, unsupervised 5-band composites (`H71`, `APFL`, `ROD`, `POL3`) fixed spatial range (`52.7%–57.1%` at `> 20 px`, `15%–25%` overlap) by abandoning supervised nonlinear tree learning, losing `-0.09` to `-0.10` `HOLDOUT-DTI` (`0.049–0.058` vs `H35 ~ 0.148`).

2. **Azimuthal Blindness & Unit Mismatch in Omitted Families `A` and `C` (`IR-25-POTENTIAL-FIELD-UNIT-MISMATCH`)**:
   - Incumbent `H35` (`BASE = "BDE"` + `EXTRAS` + `H27_tip` + `H34`) excludes **all of Family `A` and Family `C`**.
   - Byte inspection on eroded `footprint` proves `18_iso_grav_anom_hg` correlates `r = +0.9484` with the **East–West directional derivative** `10 * dg/dx` (`Azimuth 90 deg` in `mGal/km`), rather than isotropic `|grad_h g|`! Consequently, `18_iso_grav_anom_hg` has **near-zero sensitivity (`cos 90 deg = 0`) to E–W and WNW–ESE accommodation/transfer faults** (`Walker Lane` / `Humboldt` structural zones).
   - Meanwhile, `10 * hypot(dg/dx, dg/dy)` (`p99 = 7.49 mGal/km`) and `11_iso_grav_anom_vg` (`p99 = 4.24 mGal/km`) are in **identical physical units (`mGal/km`)**, enabling exact unit-consistent isotropic gravity analytic signal, tilt derivative (`THDR_g`), and Phillips full-tensor differential curvature (`K_diff`). Similarly, `10 * hypot(dT/dx, dT/dy)` (`nT/km`) and `1000 * 09_tmi_vg` (`nT/km`, since `09_tmi_vg` is in `nT/m`) restore exact magnetic tilt scaling.

3. **Strict Quarantine Verification**:
   - `external/gdr_qfaults_traces.csv` (`IR-25-QFAULTS-CSV-LEAKAGE`) and `dist_known_fault_px` inside `gdr_wellspring_in_footprint.csv` / `gdr_volcanic_vents_in_footprint.csv` are strictly forbidden and untouched.

## 3. Frozen Experimental Protocol (`H72`)

### 3.1 Experiment 1 (`H72-1`: `PTTG-DR` Screen on Untouched Draws `80, 81`)
- **Output directory:** `evidence/h72_1_screen/` (must not exist pre-run; `design.json` written before fit).
- **Folds x Draws:** `4` spatial quadrant folds (`NW=0, NE=1, SW=2, SE=3`) x `2` untouched draws (`80, 81`) = `8` buffered hide-and-recover cells (`alpha=0.2, beta=0.8`, `R=3.0 px = 300 m` triangular kernel, whole fault components withheld, `d.visible` strictly separated).
- **Leakage Canary (Protocol Rule 4):** Before fitting in each cell, every `PTTG` column (`PTTG_grav_diffcurv`, `PTTG_grav_thdr_unit`, `PTTG_transtension_loc`, `PTTG_concealed_transfer`) is tested standalone via `gems25.validation.feature_canary` (checking both value AUC and missingness-indicator AUC). Hard abort if any `canary_auc > 0.90`.
- **Paired Arms Evaluated in Every Cell:**
  1. `CTRL_H35`: Exact same-run incumbent (`BDE + EXTRAS + H27_tip + H34`, `sapd2.4`, `kfrac=0.035`).
  2. `CTRL_BLIND_BD`: Same-run catalogue-free supervised HGB control (`BD + EXTRAS + H34`, zero Family `E` or `H27_tip` columns, emitted with `d_vis > 2.0 px` exclusion, `sapd2.8`, `kfrac=0.030`).
  3. `A1_PTTG_H35`: `CTRL_H35 + PTTG (4 cols)` (`sapd2.4`, `kfrac=0.035`) — isolates pure `PTTG` multi-physics lift on the `H35` detector.
  4. `A2_PTTG_BLIND`: `CTRL_BLIND_BD + PTTG (4 cols)` (`d_vis > 2.0 px`, `sapd2.8`, `kfrac=0.030`) — isolates pure `PTTG` multi-physics lift in the 100% catalogue-free supervised regime.
  5. `A3_PTTG_DEAPRON`: **De-Aproned Dual-Regime `PTTG` Discovery**: rank-calibrated blend `0.75 * rank(p_A2_PTTG_BLIND) + 0.25 * rank(p_tip_PTTG)` (where `p_tip_PTTG` adds only strike-directed `H27_tip` + `H30_bridge`, excluding isotropic `E_dist`, `E_dens5`, `E_dens20`, `E_dens50`), emitted with `d_vis > 2.0 px` exclusion, `sapd2.8`, `kfrac=0.030`.
- **Diagnostics Recorded per Arm & Cell:**
  - Standard `HOLDOUT-DTI` (overall mean, bootstrap 95% CI, per-fold means),
  - **Off-apron far-field `HOLDOUT-DTI` (`d_vis > 9 px`)** (evaluating withheld test faults beyond the `900 m` catalogue apron alongside emitted dots at `d_vis > 9 px`),
  - Emitted dot strata (`<= 2 px`, `2–9 px`, `9–20 px`, `> 20 px`, median `d_vis`).

### 3.2 Branching Rule for Experiment 2 (`Draws 82, 83`, Max `<= 3` Experiments Total)
1. **If `A1_PTTG_H35` or `A3_PTTG_DEAPRON` passes the frozen screen gate vs `CTRL_H35`** (`mean paired gain > +0.001`, `>= 3/4` folds positive, `worst fold >= -0.010`): run **Confirmation (`evidence/h72_1_confirm/`, draws `82, 83`)** on the identical frozen specification.
2. **Otherwise (if neither beats `CTRL_H35` on screen draws `80, 81`)**: record `H72-1` vs `CTRL_H35` honestly as negative for slot promotion, and run **Experiment 2 (`H72-2 VCCD+RBDT` + `PTTG`, `evidence/h72_2_screen/`, draws `82, 83`)** testing whether adding Rank #2's **Volcanic/Playa Cover-Opacity Switching (`VCCD`) + Pluvial-Shoreline-Vetoed 3DEP Scarp Asymmetry (`RBDT`)** lifts both `CTRL_H35` (`B1_FULL_H35`) and the De-Aproned Dual-Regime detector (`B2_DEAPRON_FULL`). If Experiment 2 passes screen on `82, 83`, confirm on `84, 85` (Experiment 3, final allowed experiment); otherwise stop immediately at 2 experiments!
