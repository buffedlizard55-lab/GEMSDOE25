### Why might B2's reported 0.2778 be higher?

The pixel calculation shows B2 is the D2.8 mask with 6,436 detections at distance ≤200 m from the mapped catalogue removed. The reported D2.8 and B2 scores are distinct owner claims, not a paired organizer experiment. Under this repository's distance-tolerant Tversky implementation, nearby detections can redundantly cover the same truth while adding false-positive cost; pruning may improve the credit/cost trade-off if the removed detections carried little marginal hidden-truth credit. It may also harm performance where real new faults are near mapped traces. Catalogue-hidden proxy holdouts cannot by themselves validate a blanket off-catalogue pruning rule. Therefore neither the reported score, the observed deletion, nor the associated numerical difference proves the mechanism or a future win.

The brief's 0.3195 and 0.4413 leading-score statements conflict and have no organizer receipt here. Neither is identified as the current leaderboard. Projections are not scores.
