## 3. Frozen Mathematical Specification of `H75-1` Static Sidecar (`src/gems25/h75_dchb.py`)

All five columns in `H75_DCHB_NAMES` are static `float32` in `[0, 1]` over the `5,167,373` footprint pixels (`fi`), computed strictly from unlabeled survey inputs (**zero** access to `labels.tif`, `existing_faults.tif`, `gdr_qfaults_traces.csv`, `derived_sgmc_faults_100m_u8.tif`, or `dist_known_fault_px`). Let $\operatorname{pct}(X)$ map $(X - p_1)/(p_{99} - p_1)$ onto `[0, 1]` using finite percentiles inside the `4 px`-eroded footprint interior (`foot_core`).

1. **`H75_darcy_seepage_barrier` (100% non-LiDAR, long-wavelength piezometric barrier):**
   * Smooth `nearest_fill(12_det_elev)` with $\sigma_{\text{head}} = 30.0\text{ px}$ (`3.0 km` water-table head surface $h_w = G_{30}(z_{\text{det}})$); define unit down-head Darcy flow vector $\hat{\mathbf{u}}_w = -\nabla h_w / (\|\nabla h_w\| + 10^{-5})$ and regional hydraulic gradient magnitude $g_w = \operatorname{pct}(\|\nabla h_w\|)$.
   * Compute subsurface aquitard/basement steps on `c = log(1 + max(nearest_fill(17_cond_surf), 0))` and `b = log(1 + max(nearest_fill(15_depth_to_base_surf), 0))` at $\sigma = 2.0\text{ px}$: let $\mathbf{g}_c = \nabla G_{2.0}(c)$, $\mathbf{g}_b = \nabla G_{2.0}(b)$.
   * Positive upslope-to-downslope cross-fault barrier alignment (`Darcy` flow impinging across rising smectite gouge conductivity or abrupt basement-depth change):
     $$I_{\text{Darcy}} = g_w \cdot \operatorname{pct}\!\big(\max(0, \hat{\mathbf{u}}_w \cdot \mathbf{g}_c) + 0.5\,\|\hat{\mathbf{u}}_w \cdot \mathbf{g}_b\|\big) \cdot \sqrt{\operatorname{pct}(\|\mathbf{g}_c\|)\,\operatorname{pct}(\|\mathbf{g}_b\|) + 10^{-4}},$$
     $$\texttt{H75\_darcy\_seepage\_barrier} = \operatorname{pct}\!\big(\operatorname{ridge\_strength}(I_{\text{Darcy}}, 1.5)\big).$$

2. **`H75_uth_sinter_emergence` (100% non-LiDAR, carbonate-sinter `U/Th` crest $\times$ quartz-enthalpy upflow):**
   * Dequantize `UTh` (`external/geodawn_extensions_u8.tif` band 3) and `Th` (`external/geodawn_rad_u8.tif` band 2); define authigenic uranium-over-thorium carbonate/travertine index $A_{\text{UTh}} = \operatorname{pct}(\text{UTh}) \cdot (1.0 - 0.5\,\operatorname{pct}(\text{Th}))$, and sharpen to `150 m` radiometric sinter crests $R_{\text{UTh}} = \operatorname{pct}(\operatorname{ridge\_strength}(A_{\text{UTh}}, 1.5))$.
   * From `external/gdr_wellspring_in_footprint.csv` using **strict allowlist** `["row", "col", "layer", "thermalclass", "temp_c", "geothermquartz_c"]` (`ValueError` raised if `dist_known_fault_px` is present), compute silica-quartz thermal excess $\Delta T_q = \operatorname{clip}(\max(T_{\text{quartz}}, T_{\text{meas}}) - 15.0, 0.0, 220.0)$ weighted `1.5×` at natural springs (`layer` starting with `"spring_"`), smooth with $\sigma = 12.0\text{ px}$ (`1.2 km` upflow halo $Q_{\text{up}}$):
     $$\texttt{H75\_uth\_sinter\_emergence} = \operatorname{pct}\!\Big(R_{\text{UTh}} \cdot \big(0.30 + 0.70\sqrt{\operatorname{pct}(Q_{\text{up}} + 0.5\,\texttt{H75\_darcy\_seepage\_barrier})}\big)\Big).$$

3. **`H75_oblique_piedmont_inflection` (Shoreline-deconfounded cross-contour break-in-slope):**
   * Compute macro-contour spin-2 director $(c_{\text{mac}}, s_{\text{mac}})$ of `12_det_elev` ($\sigma_{\text{grad}} = 25\text{ px}$, $\sigma_{\text{tensor}} = 10\text{ px}$, `2.5 km` basin-margin contour) and tilt-invariant `100 m` DEM Hessian ridge director $(c_{\text{dem}}, s_{\text{dem}})$ + crest strength $w_{\text{dem}} = \operatorname{pct}(\operatorname{ridge\_strength}(z_{\text{det}}, 1.5))$ via `gems25.h72_cf`.
   * Where `L_valid > 0.5` and `L_coh100 >= 0.20`, form blended micro-director $(c_{\text{mic}}, s_{\text{mic}})$ and amplitude $w_{\text{mic}}$ using `1 m` LiDAR (`step_max, coh100, strike`); where `L_valid == 0`, fall back cleanly to `100 m` DEM $(c_{\text{dem}}, s_{\text{dem}}), w_{\text{dem}}$ so `100%` of the footprint has a finite cross-contour inflection signal!
   * Compute exact **cross-contour obliquity** (orthogonal to contour-parallel Lake Lahontan wave-cut benches!):
     $$\sin^2(\theta_{\text{mic}} - \theta_{\text{mac}}) = \tfrac{1}{2}\big(1.0 - \operatorname{clip}(c_{\text{mic}} c_{\text{mac}} + s_{\text{mic}} s_{\text{mac}}, -1.0, 1.0)\big),$$
     weighted by alluvial/piedmont setting $W_{\text{pied}} = 0.25 + 0.75\,\operatorname{pct}(\log(1 + \max(h_b, 0)))$:
     $$\texttt{H75\_oblique\_piedmont\_inflection} = \operatorname{pct}\!\big(w_{\text{mic}} \cdot \sin^2(\theta_{\text{mic}} - \theta_{\text{mac}}) \cdot W_{\text{pied}}\big).$$

4. **`H75_riedel_obliquity_shear` (En-échelon Riedel $R$-shear $\pm 17^\circ$ resonance):**
   * Compute `2.0 km` subsurface basement-corridor spin-2 director $(c_{\text{cor}}, s_{\text{cor}})$ from `13_iso_grav_anom` ($\sigma_{\text{grad}} = 15\text{ px}$, $\sigma_{\text{tensor}} = 6\text{ px}$). Rotate by $2\delta_R = \pm 34^\circ$ ($\delta_R = \pm 17^\circ$ Byerlee Riedel shear angle):
     $$c_{\pm} = c_{\text{cor}}\cos 34^\circ \mp s_{\text{cor}}\sin 34^\circ,\qquad s_{\pm} = s_{\text{cor}}\cos 34^\circ \pm c_{\text{cor}}\sin 34^\circ.$$
   * Resonance against $(c_{\text{mic}}, s_{\text{mic}})$:
     $$R_{17} = \max_{s \in \{+,-\}} \tfrac{1}{2}\big(1.0 + \operatorname{clip}(c_{\text{mic}} c_s + s_{\text{mic}} s_s, -1.0, 1.0)\big),$$
     weighted by geodetic wrench strain rate $S_{\text{sh}} = \operatorname{pct}(\max(\texttt{07\_geod\_shearrate}, 0))$:
     $$\texttt{H75\_riedel\_obliquity\_shear} = \operatorname{pct}\!\big(w_{\text{mic}} \cdot R_{17} \cdot (0.20 + 0.80\,S_{\text{sh}})\big).$$

5. **`H75_destriped_blakely_contact` (100% non-LiDAR, E–W flight-line-destriped Blakely–Simpson contact spine):**
   * Remove `200–400 m` E–W (`090°`) GeoDAWN tie-line corrugation from `02_rtp` and `TMI_up150` (`external/geodawn_extensions_u8.tif` band 4): $F_{\text{ds}} = F - G_{(0, 14)}(F - G_{(2.0, 0)}(F))$.
   * Compute Blakely & Simpson (1986) maximum-horizontal-gradient Hessian contact crests $C_{\text{mag}} = \operatorname{pct}(\operatorname{ridge\_strength}(\|\nabla G_{1.5}(\text{RTP}_{\text{ds}})\|, 1.2))$, $C_{\text{up}} = \operatorname{pct}(\operatorname{ridge\_strength}(\|\nabla G_{2.0}(\text{UP150}_{\text{ds}})\|, 1.2))$, and gravity contact crest $C_{\text{grav}} = \operatorname{pct}(\operatorname{ridge\_strength}(\|\nabla G_{2.0}(g_{\text{iso}})\|, 1.5))$:
     $$\texttt{H75\_destriped\_blakely\_contact} = \operatorname{pct}\!\Big(\sqrt{C_{\text{mag}}\,C_{\text{up}}} \cdot (0.35 + 0.65\,C_{\text{grav}})\Big).$$

## 4. Frozen Architecture & Experimental Design (`<= 3` Experiments, Virgin Draws `120–123`)

### Corridor-Gated Dual-Regime Cross-Fit (`H75_CG_BLIND`, Frozen $\gamma = 0.60$, `gap_scale = 0.95`)
Within every `(fold, draw)` cell (and in 4-quadrant `LOQO` full-footprint staging), using **identical training indices `(train_idx, y_tr)`** and shared `HistGradientBoostingClassifier(random_state=seed, **gems25.experiment.HGB_PARAMS)`:

Predicts smooth subsurface/hydrogeologic corridor probability $P_{\text{cor}}(x)$ across `100%` of pixels (`0` Family-B columns, `0` Family-E columns).
2. **`M_trace` (`83` static multi-scale shoreline-deconfounded columns):** `ABCD` (`64`) + `EXTRAS` (`7`) + `H34` (`3`) + `H74_RMPS` (`4`) + `H75_DCHB` (`5`) = `83` columns (`0` Family-E columns). Predicts `100 m`-sharp trace probability $P_{\text{trace}}(x)$ on `L_valid == 1`.
3. **`M_gap` (`70` static non-LiDAR columns):** Exact `M_trace` column set minus the `13` `L_*` 1-m LiDAR columns (`83 - 13 = 70` columns, `100%` observed across `L_valid == 0`). Predicts `100 m` DEM/radiometric/hydrogeologic trace probability $P_{\text{gap}}(x)$ across `L_valid == 0`.
4. **Frozen Corridor-Gated Coupling (`combine_corridor_gated_scores`, no hyperparameter tuning):**
