## 1. The reported portal error — local root-cause inference (COMPUTED)
The first GEMSDOE25 "recommended" file used an invented footprint: **2,344,929 of the 5,167,373 owner-mirror template-footprint pixels were NaN**, 2,832,257 pixels outside it were finite,
and it emitted 1,249,834 positive pixels (`evidence/old_gems25_tif_forensics.json`). NaN fails every `0 ≤ x ≤ 1` test, consistent with the reported message "Predicted values must be in
range [0, 1]". INFERENCE: this is a data-supported explanation, not a DrivenData confirmation. The validator is not public, and the template is an integrity-pinned owner mirror, not organizer-authenticated. The new writer takes the footprint from that mirror and locally rejects NaN/out-of-range values.

## 2. Local identity of the raster labelled 0.2477 (score unverified)
* The owner-mirrored raster labelled 0.2477 is **pixel-for-pixel `dot_thin(H19-5, 1.5)`**: 60,069 of 121,131 pixels (49.6 %), zero NaN inside the mirror's footprint, none on a catalogue pixel (`evidence/why_0_2477.json`). This verifies a transformation among local mirrors, not the competition score or organizer file identity.
* Identity: `DTI = TP_w / (0.2 (TP_w + FP_w) + 0.8 |G|)`. A pixel pays for itself only if (extra credit)/(extra false-positive mass) > `0.2·DTI/(1−0.2·DTI)` ≈ **0.052** at a hypothetical DTI of 0.2477.
* Conditional truth-size calculations from user/owner-reported, **unverified** inputs: blind-lattice claim 0.0904 → **12,503 px**; H19-5→dotted-claim pair 0.1922→0.2477 → **12,769 px** (2 % apart). These are not independent live-score verifications. The proxy lattice result is 0.0861, but agreement with an unverified input does not authenticate it.
* Geometric credit retention in the local masks is 0.853 under the assumed truth geometry. The fitted model (`evidence/emission_model.json`) is conditional on unverified score claims and assumed geometry; it is not an estimate backed by organizer receipts.
* An unverified out-of-sample claim pair (solid H25 0.1280 → dotted H28 0.1839): model output **0.1672** (difference −0.017). Since both inputs are unverified, this is a sensitivity check, not a validated live out-of-sample test.

## 4. Fractional factorial 2^(5−1), Resolution V (COMPUTED, `evidence/factorial/results.md`)
16 runs × 8 hide-and-recover cells, response = exact sparse-regime DTI after a fixed emission. Lenth ME = 0.0139.

| Factor | Family | Main effect | Folds + | Class (frozen rule) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E | catalogue geometry (visible faults only) | **+0.0610** | 4/4 | matters alone |
| B | DEM curvature / scarp (100 m + 3DEP LiDAR descriptors) | **+0.0253** | 4/4 | matters alone |
| D | thermal / geochemical (radiometrics, conductivity, GDR springs & wells) | +0.0044 | 4/4 | inert (below ME; small but consistent) |
| A | potential-field gradients | −0.0027 | 1/4 | inert |
| C | strain / seismicity | −0.0109 | 0/4 | inert by rule (consistently negative, just under −ME) |

* **Sparsity of effects holds:** E and B carry **94 %** of the effect energy (E 80 %, B 14 %); the largest of the ten interactions is |−0.0050| (BC), below the ME. **No family "matters only in combination"** by the frozen rule.
* B×E = −0.0043: the two active families partly substitute (both encode fault-prone terrain). The model-predicted best corner, **B+E alone (0.145)**, lies in the unexecuted half-fraction and is tested in the confirmation replicate.
* AUC effects agree (E +0.204, B +0.065, BE −0.037). Hug-share (emission within 3 px of visible faults) is *lower* with E (−0.034): E does not make the emission cling to known faults.
* Same-harness references: random-score ridge/dots **0.066**, unsupervised topographic ridge 0.034, unsupervised potential-field ridge 0.040. Supervised surfaces with E/B reach 0.11–0.14. A 100 m tree model on potential-field, strain or earthquake bands carries **no transferable signal across quadrants** (A alone AUC 0.50, C alone 0.49).

**What this does not say.** Potential fields are not useless for faults: at 100 m, with generic trees and blocked validation they did not transfer, and concealed faults need dedicated processing (H26-3). Family E very likely flatters the proxy
(faults cluster; hidden and visible components are neighbours); an owner-reported live-score record (not independently verified; ≥45 % catalogue-adjacent emission is reported at ≤ 0.046) is consistent with proximity alone failing.
