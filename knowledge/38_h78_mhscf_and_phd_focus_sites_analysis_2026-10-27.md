## What explains the reported 0.2778?

Among the supplied historical claims, GEMSDOE32's B2 has the highest **OWNER-REPORTED** value, 0.2778; no organizer receipt was found. Independently recomputing the nine pinned masks shows that B2 is exactly GEMSDOE25 D2.8 with 6,436 predicted pixels at distance ≤200 m from the mirrored catalogue removed; GEMSDOE36's mask contains B2 plus 1,200 pixels. GEMSDOE25 and 30 have identical positive masks. GEMSDOE28's highest supplied file is the 0.2710 Rung30 raster, **not** the 0.2708 R1 file used in some older comparisons; Rung30 and GEMSDOE31 differ, whereas GEMSDOE28's lower-scoring R1 has the same positive mask as GEMSDOE31. [Nine-file audit](../evidence/focus_winners_reaudit_2026-10-27.json) · [pinned-source verification](../evidence/focus_source_links_2026-10-27.json).

Thinning and catalogue-adjacent pruning could reduce redundant false-positive mass under the local 300-m Tversky-style metric while retaining useful coverage. That is a plausible mechanism, **not proof of hidden-label causation**. We do not know whether a new method would beat 0.2778, and neither the owner-reported scores nor the local scorer have been independently authenticated by the organizer.

## This continuation

Four distinct geological hypotheses, their physical layers, non-fault mimics, differences from existing implementations, costs and verified official sources were frozen in [the preregistration](39_lane_hypotheses_and_preflight_2026-10-27.md), before the fresh preflight. The highest-ranked continuation was an independent subsurface-root expert × scarp expert, not a wholly untried product-of-experts architecture; an earlier H36 already used a related dual-head structure. The user's reference to a separate “single method paragraph” was not uniquely identifiable, so the prior H74 handoff was used provisionally without claiming owner confirmation.

The geometry preflight found 6,514 eligible pixels outside the r13 three-pixel halo. A 37,654-dot candidate must overlap by at least `(37,654 − 6,514) / 37,654 = 82.70%`, above 70%. The frozen stop rule therefore stopped the run **before any model fit, feature leakage canary, hide-and-recover experiment, candidate surface, placement or new TIFF**. No adjustment of the budget or density exemption was used. A maximum of 21,713 dots is merely not ruled out by this one necessary condition; it is not a unique, competitive or approved candidate.
