## 1. Why GEMSDOE32 h33-2-b2 scored 0.2778 — PhD Forensic (HOLDOUT-DTI vs OWNER-REPORTED)

- **Lineage (COMPUTED, pixel-exact):** `19GEMSDOE` H19-5 (121,131 px, OWNER-REPORTED 0.1922, unsupervised 6-channel RankGauss Hessian ridge) → `GEMSDOE24` D1.5 `dot_thin(H19-5,1.5)` (60,069 px, 0.2477, -61,062 redundant neighbours) → `GEMSDOE25/30` D2.8 `dot_thin(H19-5,2.4)` (44,090 px, 0.2600, -15,979) → `GEMSDOE28/31` R1 `D2.8 & EDT(~cat)>1` (40,199 px, 0.2708, deletes 3,891 d≤1 px collar dots at 0.0010 credit/px) → **`GEMSDOE32 B2 D2.8 & EDT>2` (37,654 px, 0.2778, deletes additional 2,545 at 1<d≤2 px, 0.0050 credit/px)**. Verified `XOR=0` vs `src/gems25/thinning.py::dot_thin` and distance-transform masks.

- **Metric mechanism (OFFICIAL):** Competition DTI per page 967: `DTI = TP_w / (0.2 M + 0.8 N)` with triangular `k(d)=max(1-d/300m,0)`. For binary predictions, adding/substituting ΔM improves D iff `ΔTP_w/ΔM > 0.2·D`. At D=0.2600 break-even `τ=0.0520`; at D=0.2708 `τ=0.0542`; at D=0.2778 `τ=0.0556`. Deleted shells earned 0.0010 (52× below) and 0.0050 (11× below) — pure denominator reduction.

- **Geological reason:** Qfaults/INGENIOUS vector traces are 1-px (100 m) polylines offset 100–200 m from maximum-slope scarp; unsupervised H19-5 ridges fire 1–2 px parallel to mapped faults where no unmapped fault exists; hidden faults (blind step-overs, piedmont scarps, accommodation splays) lie >200 m away, so pruning retains coverage while cutting FP. Verified by SGMC off-catalogue instrument: 70.9% of D2.8 mass within 300 m of catalogue — flank mass dominates.

- **Why best among focus sites:** All other focus winners keep low-credit flank dots. GEMSDOE31 R1 is B=1 only (0.2708); GEMSDOE28 variants (0.2707–0.2710) use Euler/tilt geometry not flank pruning; GEMSDOE36 Anderson Pinn (0.2750) is B2 ∪ 1,200 extra dots (0.0126/px < τ=0.0588, dilutes). GEMSDOE33 h33d (0.2632) re-adds 1,666 near-tip dots, dropping -0.0076 vs R1. Only B2 removes both shells.

- **Ceiling:** Uniform N≈12,691 model shows 0.2778 needs TP_w≈4,871 (38.4% weighted recall); 0.3195 needs 5,650 (44.5%); 0.4413 needs 7,804 (61.5%). Further B=3 prune (2<d≤3) earns ~0.064/px >τ, so B3 would hurt; sweep of H19-5 spacing peaks at d=2.4 (0.251 model ceiling). Beating 0.2778 requires new detector credit density, not emission trimming.
