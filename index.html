Local audit of the reported 0.2477 raster (score unverified)

  1. The mask is exactly dot_thin(H19-5, 1.5) — pixel-for-pixel a deterministic subset of the H19-5-labelled raster: 60,069 of 121,131 pixels kept (49.6 %), none on a catalogue pixel, zero NaN inside the footprint. This verifies the raster transformation, not the reported DTI.
  2. Metric arithmetic: DTI = TPw / (0.2·(TPw+FPw) + 0.8·|G|). Thinning keeps ≈85 % of the geometric credit estimate while removing ≈50 % of the emitted pixels.
  3. Unverified score inputs: the 0.0904 lattice and 0.1922→0.2477 values are supplied/owner-reported claims. Under those assumptions, estimated |G| is ≈12,503 px and each emitted pixel needs ≥0.052 credit per unit of false-positive mass. No organizer page or receipt was accessed.

H28 metric-model calibration conditional on owner-reported claims — and the ceiling of the current surface family

  1. Conditional on owner-reported anchors, a uniform-π truth model has no finite solution. For four of the group's own surfaces the published DTI has no solution in the truth count under a uniform truth. Under pi ∝ exp(-d(H19-5)/1.85 px) all five anchors imply one count — lattice-s5 12,498, h19-5 12,472, h19-4 12,893, h16-1 13,358, d1-5 12,691 — so model-implied N̂ = 12,691 pixels in the hidden truth (spread 5.3 %), agreeing with the blind-lattice-only estimate 12,348 and the earlier independent 12,503.
  2. The fitted model matches selected owner-reported anchors conditionally; this is not score verification. Predicted vs reported: lattice-s5 0.0914 / 0.0904 · h19-5 0.1903 / 0.1922 · d1-5 0.2437 / 0.2477. Monte-Carlo of the published metric agrees with the analytic expectation to ≤0.0020.
  3. Within the tested H19-5 dotting sweep and selected model, the earlier D2.8 file was the modelled ceiling of that single-surface family. Sweeping dot_thin(H19-5, d) over d ∈ [1.8, 4.0] peaks at d = 2.4 px, 44,090 px → 0.25104. A kernel-disjoint 6 px design (zero redundancy) reaches only 0.19332 and beats d1-5 in 0 of 9 sensitivity cells.
  4. Conditional model arithmetic against the unverified 0.3195 claim. Matching that owner-reported number at this budget would need 1.29× the credit density (0.1400 vs 0.1084 per emitted pixel); this is not a verified score comparison or proof that all emission changes are exhausted.
